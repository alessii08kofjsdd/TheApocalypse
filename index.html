02 / THE STORY

THE STORY

THE NIGHT
BEGINS LIKE
ANY OTHER.

The protagonist leaves for work expecting another ordinary night.

During the shift, disturbing reports begin appearing. Emergency services are overwhelmed. Something violent is spreading through the city.

A mysterious infection is transforming people into extremely aggressive beings.

The first recorded case appears in the protagonist's city.

Then the number of cases begins to rise.

When the shift ends, the protagonist calls an Uber to return home.

He enters the house.

And discovers that his wife has been infected.

From this moment, the real experience begins.

THE GAME

THE APOCALYPSE is a first-person survival horror experience focused on atmosphere, exploration, tension and survival.

The game combines cinematic storytelling with exploration, environmental puzzles, limited resources and enemy AI.

The player is not a hero.

The player is simply trying to survive.
